The dashboard is
decorated, not designed.

SCOPE    6 pages, 14 components
FILES    dashboard/app, dashboard/components
LOC      ~1,900 lines of CSS alone
VERDICT Salvageable structure, replace surface

§ 01

Diagnosis

Five root causes.
Everything downstream
is a symptom.

  1. 01

    Every section shouts. Nothing speaks.

    Eyebrow + title + badge row + hero band + sparkline + dot + glow on every block. When everything is "Command Pulse / Live Attention Feed / 12 High-or-Critical Actions", the operator's eye has nowhere to land. There is no quiet space against which an alert can register as alert.

  2. 02

    It's a read-only museum.

    Across 6 pages, there is one real interaction: the "Force Retry" button on a queue card. No filtering, no sorting, no bulk actions, no resolve/assign/snooze on the action cards, no inline drill-down. An ops dashboard with no ops verbs is a poster.

  3. 03

    The hierarchy lies.

    Decorative MetricCards (with fake sparklines from a literal fallbackTrendMap) are visually heavier than the actual failed-payment alert. Hero bands repeat the page title in slightly different words. Section eyebrows repeat the nav label. The loudest pixels are the least informative.

  4. 04

    Same data, four widgets.

    The Overview shows accounts as a Revenue Risk list AND an Account Health panel AND a Discrepancy table — all keyed on the same accounts. Recent events appear on Overview and Events. Open ops actions appear on Overview, Actions, and per-account. The user must reconcile near-duplicate views in their head.

  5. 05

    The product narrative is missing.

    SyncCore reconciles billing ↔ CRM and recovers failed payments. The UI shows that work as 6 disconnected list pages instead of a flow: event arrives → discrepancy detected → action proposed → operator acts → outcome logged. A pipeline product needs a pipeline view.

§ 02

Visual vocabulary

What the design system
currently encodes —
and what it should.

Pattern What's wrong Replace with

Eyebrow + title

.section-header__eyebrow

Every section heading has an uppercase kicker ("Command pulse", "Triage", "Observability") repeating or re-naming what the title already says. Adds a second tier of noise to scan past.

One H2 per section. If the section needs a kicker, the section probably needs to be a route, a tab, or merged with its neighbor.

Hero band

.hero-band

A backdrop-blurred, gradient-edged "section opener" that restates the page title and shows 3 stats that are either duplicates of the metric cards (Queue page) or pure padding ("Triage surface"). Pure decoration.

Delete. If a page genuinely needs context, put it in a single line of body text under the H1 — not a 140px-tall glass panel.

Metric cards w/ sparklines

.metric-card

Trends come from a hardcoded fallbackTrendMap — they are lies. Tone badges ("ok", "alert", "watch") redundantly label what the color already says. Each card is ~180px tall to hold a fake chart.

Either ship a real time series (with real data + axis + comparison period) or kill the sparkline and use a tight one-line KPI strip. No middle ground.

Badge zoo

badge × 7 variants

SeverityBadge, StatusBadge, SourceBadge, ActionStatusBadge, plain Badge — each pill rounded with a glow shadow and leading dot. The Overview "badge-row" stacks 3 of them under the H1; an Action Card carries 4 at once. Visual noise > information density.

Two badge types only: severity (semantic color, no glow) and tag (neutral, no color). Status becomes an inline icon + text, not a pill. Remove dot prefixes — they double the visual weight of every label.

Dark glass surfaces

backdrop-filter, glow shadows

Translucent surfaces, radial accent glows on the body, blur(22px), shadow flares per severity on hover. Reads as "designer dashboard demo" not as a tool people work in for 6 hours a day.

Pick one: light-mode operator console (recommended for revops) or flat dark mode with zero glass, zero glow. No radial gradients on the body.

Split-grid everywhere

.split-grid--two, --golden

The Overview is 4 stacked 2-column splits. Each cell is independent — there is no relationship across the seam. The reader's eye zig-zags. Sections that should be primary (the failed-payment alert) sit at 50% width next to a tertiary list.

A real editorial grid: one full-bleed primary, one 2-up secondary row, the rest as a single long list. Width = importance.

§ 05

Rebuild plan

Three waves, scoped
to ship value early.

Wave 01

Strip
the noise

~3 days · no new patterns

  • ·Delete all .hero-band instances. Page-headers keep the H1 only.
  • ·Remove eyebrow kickers from SectionHeader. Section title = H2, no decoration.
  • ·Kill the fake sparklines + tone-label badges on MetricCard.
  • ·Collapse the 7 badge variants to 2 (severity + tag). Drop leading dots and glow shadows.
  • ·Switch from translucent glass surfaces to flat opaque. Remove body radial gradients.
  • ·De-duplicate Overview: remove "Live attention feed" OR "Open ops actions" — pick one.

Wave 02

Make it
operable

~1.5 wks · new patterns 02, 03, 04

  • ·Build Triage row (pattern 02). Replace ActionCard grid + queue-card grid + Overview "command pulse".
  • ·Build Diff cell (pattern 03). Reuse on Reconciler, Account detail, Action detail.
  • ·Build Account row + sparkbar (pattern 04). Replaces the two duplicate panels on Overview.
  • ·Wire real verbs: resolve, snooze, assign, accept-A/B, force retry. Server actions, optimistic UI.
  • ·Filter/sort/search on every list page. Pagination on Events.
  • ·Tabs on Account detail: Activity / Discrepancies / Actions / Billing.

Wave 03

Tell the
story

~1 wk · new patterns 01, 05, 06
